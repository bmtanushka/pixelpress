    Why 100 KB?

    100 KB is a sweet spot: small enough for strict upload forms and fast-loading pages, yet big enough for a sharp 1000–1500 pixel photo. It's a common limit for profile pictures, ID uploads and email attachments on slow connections.

    Because CompressZone searches for the best quality under your limit, most photos reach 100 KB without any resizing at all.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I compress several photos to 100 KB at once?

    Yes. Add as many images as you like; each one is compressed to under 100 KB, and you can download them all as a ZIP.

    Should I choose JPG or WebP for 100 KB?

    Use JPG for forms and email, since every system accepts it. For websites, WebP gives better quality at the same size.

    Is 100 KB good quality?

    Yes — for screens, a 100 KB photo at about 1200 px wide looks sharp. For printing, use a larger size.
