How to turn photos and scans into a single PDF

Updated October 19, 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer: Add your images to JPG to PDF, drag them into order, choose A4 or Letter and click Create PDF. On iPhone, the Files and Notes apps can also scan documents straight to PDF.

Getting a good scan with your phone

Place the document on a dark, flat surface in bright, even light, and hold the phone directly above it. On iPhone, the Files app (… → Scan Documents) and the Notes app can detect page edges and straighten them automatically. Many Android phones offer document scanning in Google Drive or the camera app.

Choosing page size and quality

Use A4 for most countries and US Letter in the US and Canada. Choose Balanced quality for documents you'll email or upload, and High if they'll be printed. If a form limits the PDF size, compress the photos first.

On Windows without extra software

Select the images in File Explorer, right-click and choose Print, then pick Microsoft Print to PDF as the printer. This works for simple cases, but you have less control over order and margins.

Step by step: how to convert images to a PDF

1

Add your images

Open JPG to PDF and drop in your photos or scans — JPG, PNG and iPhone HEIC all work.

2

Arrange the pages

Drag the thumbnails into the right order and remove any you don't need.

3

Create the PDF

Choose the page size, orientation, margins and quality, then click Create PDF.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make the PDF smaller?

Choose Small file quality, or compress the images first with the image compressor before creating the PDF.

Can I add more pages to an existing PDF?

Create a PDF from the new images, then combine both files with Merge PDF.

Is it safe for ID documents?

Yes — images are turned into a PDF in your browser and never uploaded.
