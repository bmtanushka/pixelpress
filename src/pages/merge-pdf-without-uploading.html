How to merge PDF files without uploading them anywhere

Updated October 19, 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer: Use a merge tool that runs in your browser, such as CompressZone's Merge PDF: add the files, put them in order and click Merge. The PDFs are combined on your device and never uploaded. On a Mac, Preview can also merge PDFs.

Why uploading PDFs is a privacy risk

The documents people merge most often — contracts, bank statements, tax forms, medical records, ID scans — are also the most sensitive. Many online PDF tools upload your files to their servers to process them. Even if they promise to delete them, you have to trust their security and policies.

Browser-based tools avoid that entirely: the merging code runs on your own computer or phone, and the files never leave it.

Merging PDFs on a Mac with Preview

Open the first PDF in Preview, choose View → Thumbnails, then drag the other PDF files into the thumbnail sidebar where you want them. Choose File → Export as PDF to save the combined file.

Merging PDFs on Windows

Windows doesn't include a built-in way to merge PDFs. A browser-based tool works in Edge or Chrome with nothing to install.

Step by step: how to merge PDF files privately

1

Open Merge PDF

Go to Merge PDF in any modern browser on your computer or phone.

2

Add the files

Drop two or more PDFs onto the page. Each file's page count is shown.

3

Set the order and merge

Drag the files into the right order and click Merge PDFs. The combined file downloads as merged.pdf.
