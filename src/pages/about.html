About CompressZone

Updated October 19, 2026

CompressZone is a free collection of online image and PDF tools, built so that your files never leave your device. Most online tools upload your files to a server. CompressZone does all the work inside your web browser, so it's faster, works offline, and keeps your photos and documents private.

Who runs CompressZone

CompressZone is built and operated by Webulex Technologies. We design and maintain every tool, write the guides, and keep the site free by showing advertising. You can reach us any time at hmlakmi@gmail.com.

How it works

  • Decoding and resizing: your browser's built-in image engine reads each file and scales it with high-quality, multi-step downsampling.
  • JPG, WebP and AVIF: encoded with the browser's native encoders in background threads (Web Workers), so the page stays responsive.
  • PNG: optimized with color quantization (UPNG.js) and DEFLATE compression, keeping transparency.
  • WebP on Safari: Safari can't save WebP natively, so CompressZone uses Google's libwebp encoder compiled to WebAssembly.
  • Never bigger: if re-encoding would make a file larger, the original is kept.
  • HEIC photos: decoded natively in Safari and with libheif (WebAssembly) in other browsers.
  • PDFs: created, merged, split and rotated with the open-source pdf-lib library, and rendered to images with Mozilla's PDF.js — the engine used in Firefox.
  • Metadata: read with exifr and removed losslessly by dropping metadata blocks from JPG, PNG and WebP files.

Privacy

Files are processed in memory on your device and discarded when you close the page. They are never uploaded, stored or seen by us. The site is supported by advertising from Google AdSense, which never has access to your files. Read the full privacy policy.

Key facts

  • Price: free, with no account or sign-up
  • Image tools: compress, compress to a target KB, resize (including exact pixels and passport sizes), convert (HEIC, PNG, JPG, WebP, Base64), crop, rotate, flip, favicon generator, EXIF viewer and remover
  • PDF tools: JPG to PDF, PDF to JPG/PNG, merge, split, rotate, organize and delete pages
  • Supported images: JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, AVIF, GIF, BMP and SVG
  • Default compression quality: 80%
  • Limits: none, apart from your device's memory
  • Works on: Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on desktop and mobile
