Contact us

We read every message and usually reply within a few working days.

CompressZone is run by Webulex Technologies.

Email: hmlakmi@gmail.com

What you can contact us about

  • Bug reports: tell us which tool you used, your browser and device, and what happened. Please don't attach private files — describe the problem instead, or share a sample file you're comfortable sending.
  • Tool suggestions: missing a format, size preset or PDF feature? Let us know what you need.
  • Privacy questions: CompressZone processes files in your browser and never uploads them. Read the privacy policy for details, or ask us anything.
  • Corrections: if something in our guides is out of date or wrong, we'd like to fix it.
  • Partnerships and press: blogs, schools and organizations are welcome to link to or recommend our tools.

Before you write

Many questions are answered in the FAQ on each tool page and in our guides. To learn how the tools work and keep your files private, see About CompressZone.
