When to flip an image

Front cameras often save selfies mirrored, so text and logos appear backwards. Flipping horizontally fixes that. Designers also mirror images to balance a layout or make a subject face into the page.

A vertical flip turns the image upside down as a reflection, which is different from rotating it 180°.

Frequently asked questions

How do I un-mirror a selfie?

Add the selfie and click Flip H. Any text in the photo will read correctly again.

Is flipping vertically the same as rotating 180°?

No. Flipping vertically mirrors the image top to bottom; rotating 180° turns it upside down without mirroring.

Does flipping reduce quality?

No pixels are lost. Choose PNG or Best quality to avoid any re-encoding loss.

Can I flip several images at once?

The flip tool edits one image at a time so you can preview the result. Add the next image with New image when you're done.

What format is the flipped image saved in?

By default the same format as the original (JPG, PNG or WebP). You can also choose a different format before downloading.
