    Best Instagram image sizes

    Instagram displays images 1080 pixels wide. Uploading at exactly the right size avoids surprise crops and extra compression by the app. The 4:5 portrait size (1080 × 1350) fills the most screen space in the feed, square posts are 1080 × 1080, and stories and reels use 9:16 (1080 × 1920).

    Use Fit inside if you want to post a landscape photo without cropping it.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the best image size for Instagram?

    1080 × 1350 pixels (4:5 portrait) for feed posts, 1080 × 1080 for squares, and 1080 × 1920 for stories and reels.

    How do I post a full landscape photo on Instagram without cropping?

    Choose 1080 × 1080 or 1080 × 1350 with Fit inside. The whole photo is kept and white bars are added.

    Will Instagram still compress my photo?

    Instagram re-compresses everything, but uploading at the exact size and high quality gives the sharpest result.
