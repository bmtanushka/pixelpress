Delete PDF Pages
Remove what you don't need

Remove blank, duplicate or private pages from a PDF before you share it.

    How to delete pages from a PDF

    1

    Add a PDF

    Drop the PDF onto the box above to see all of its pages.

    2

    Mark pages to remove

    Click the bin icon under each page you want to delete. Click it again to restore the page.

    3

    Save

    Click Save PDF to download the new file without those pages.

    Remove pages before sharing

    Sending a whole PDF when only part of it is needed can expose private information. Removing pages takes seconds here, and because it happens in your browser, the document is never uploaded anywhere.

    The remaining pages are copied exactly, with no loss of quality.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I delete a page from a PDF without Acrobat?

    Add the PDF here, click the bin under the page and click Save PDF. No software or account is needed.

    Can I delete several pages at once?

    Yes. Mark as many pages as you like, then save once.

    Is the original file changed?

    No. A new PDF is downloaded; your original file stays as it is.

    Can I delete pages from a password-protected PDF?

    Protected PDFs may not save correctly. Remove the password in a PDF reader first, then delete the pages here.

    How do I keep only a few pages instead?

    Use Split PDF: select the pages you want to keep and click Extract.
