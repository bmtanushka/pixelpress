    How to rearrange PDF pages

    1

    Add a PDF

    Drop a PDF onto the box above to see every page as a thumbnail.

    2

    Rearrange

    Drag pages into a new order (or use the arrows), rotate them, and click the bin to remove a page.

    3

    Save

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I move a page in a PDF?

    Drag its thumbnail to the new position, or use the arrows under it, then save.

    Can I undo deleting a page?

    Yes. Deleted pages stay visible but faded until you save; click restore to bring one back.

    Does reordering affect quality?

    No. Pages are copied exactly, with text and images unchanged.

    Can I combine pages from different PDFs?

    Merge the files first with Merge PDF, then open the merged file here to reorder, rotate or delete individual pages.

    Will links and selectable text still work?

    Yes. Pages are copied as they are, so text stays selectable and searchable and links keep working.
