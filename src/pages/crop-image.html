How to crop an image

1

Add your image

Drop an image onto the box above, browse, or paste it from your clipboard.

2

Drag the box to move it and drag the handles to resize it. Pick a ratio such as 1:1 or 16:9 to lock the shape.

3

Download

Choose the output format and click Download. The exact crop size in pixels is shown below the ratios.

Choosing a crop ratio

Aspect ratios keep your crop the right shape for where it's going. Use 1:1 for profile pictures and square posts, 4:5 for Instagram portraits, 16:9 for YouTube thumbnails and presentations, 9:16 for stories and reels, and 3:2 or 4:3 for prints.

The crop uses the full-resolution original, so there's no quality loss beyond saving the file. Use the arrow keys to nudge the crop box precisely.

Frequently asked questions

Does cropping reduce image quality?

No. Cropping only removes pixels at the edges. The remaining image keeps its full resolution.

How do I crop a photo to a square?

Choose 1:1, then drag the box to frame your subject and download.

Can I crop to exact pixel dimensions?

The crop size is shown in pixels as you adjust it. To get an exact output size like 600 × 600, crop to the right ratio here, then use Resize to exact pixels.

Can I crop on my phone?

Yes. The crop box works with touch — drag inside to move and drag the corners to resize.
