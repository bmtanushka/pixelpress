    Why convert PNG to WebP?

    WebP supports transparency like PNG but uses far more efficient compression. For photographic content stored as PNG, converting to WebP often reduces the file size by 70–90%. For graphics and screenshots, savings are smaller but still significant.

    CompressZone converts PNG to lossy WebP with an alpha channel, so transparent backgrounds stay transparent. For logos and text-heavy screenshots, a quality of 90% or higher keeps edges sharp.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does WebP keep PNG transparency?

    Yes. WebP supports an alpha channel, and CompressZone preserves transparent and semi-transparent pixels when converting.

    Is WebP better than PNG?

    For websites, usually yes: WebP files are smaller and load faster. PNG is still useful for editing workflows and software that doesn't support WebP.

    What quality should I use for PNG to WebP?

    Use 80% for photos and 90–95% for logos, icons, text and screenshots, where sharp edges matter.

    Does PNG to WebP work for animated PNGs?

    Only the first frame is converted. For still images, transparency and full quality are preserved.

    Will my website support WebP images?

    All modern browsers display WebP, and platforms such as WordPress, Shopify and Squarespace accept WebP uploads.
