    Frequently asked questions

    Are my images uploaded to a server?

    No. CompressZone runs entirely in your browser. Your images are read, compressed and saved on your own device — they are never sent anywhere.

    What quality should I choose?

    The default of 80% is the industry-standard balance and works well for photos on websites and social media. Use 90%+ for print or archiving, and 60% when file size matters most.

    Why convert to WebP?

    WebP files are typically 25–35% smaller than JPG at the same visual quality, support transparency like PNG, and are supported by all modern browsers. They're ideal for faster websites.

    How does PNG compression work?

    PNG is lossless, so CompressZone reduces the number of colours (smart quantisation) to shrink the file — often by 50–80% — while keeping transparency. At 95%+ quality, PNGs are re-encoded losslessly.

    Is there a file size or count limit?

    There are no artificial limits. Very large images (over ~100 megapixels) may be limited by your device's memory.

    Does it keep EXIF / location data?

    No. Metadata such as camera details and GPS location is removed during compression — a nice bonus for privacy. Image orientation is applied automatically.
