Frequently asked questions

How do I combine multiple JPGs into one PDF?

Add all the images at once, drag them into order, and click Create PDF. Each image becomes one page.

Can I convert PNG or iPhone HEIC photos to PDF?

Yes. PNG, WebP, HEIC, GIF and BMP images are all supported.

How do I make the PDF smaller?

Choose Small file under Image quality. You can also compress the photos first with the image compressor.

Is it safe to convert ID documents?

Yes. Your images are processed in your browser and are never uploaded to any server.
