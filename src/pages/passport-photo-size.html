Common passport photo sizes

Online applications usually specify a photo size in pixels and a maximum file size. A 2 × 2 inch photo at 300 DPI is 600 × 600 pixels, used for US passports and visas. Many countries use 35 × 45 mm, which is about 413 × 531 pixels at 300 DPI.

CompressZone crops evenly from the center to the exact size and saves a JPG. It doesn't change the background or check biometric rules, so always follow your country's official photo guidelines.

Frequently asked questions

What size is a 2×2 passport photo in pixels?

At 300 DPI, 2 × 2 inches is 600 × 600 pixels. Online forms often accept between 600 × 600 and 1200 × 1200 pixels.

What is 35×45 mm in pixels?

At 300 DPI, 35 × 45 mm is about 413 × 531 pixels.

Can this tool make my photo meet official requirements?

It sets the exact pixel size and file size. Requirements such as background color, head size and lighting depend on the photo itself — check your government's official guidelines.

How do I keep my face centered?

Crop to fill keeps the center of the photo. If your face is off-center, crop it first with the Crop Image tool, then resize here.
