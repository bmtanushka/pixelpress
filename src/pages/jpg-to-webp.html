Why convert JPG to WebP?

WebP is a modern image format created by Google that produces smaller files than JPG at the same visual quality. According to Google's own comparison, lossy WebP images are 25–34% smaller than equivalent JPEGs. Smaller images make pages load faster, which improves Core Web Vitals scores and user experience.

WebP is supported by every modern browser, including Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari 14 and later. Unlike JPG, it also supports transparency.

Frequently asked questions

Does converting JPG to WebP lose quality?

Converting re-encodes the image, but at 80% quality the result looks the same as the JPG to the eye while being significantly smaller. Use the before/after slider to compare.

Do all browsers support WebP?

Yes. Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Opera and Safari (version 14 and later on macOS Big Sur and iOS 14) all display WebP images.

Can WordPress use WebP images?

Yes. WordPress has supported uploading WebP images since version 5.8.

Can I convert WebP back to JPG?

Yes — use the WebP to JPG converter. Keep your original JPGs if you might need them, since each lossy conversion can lose a little detail.
