Frequently asked questions

Does removing EXIF data reduce image quality?

Not for JPG, PNG and WebP — the metadata is removed without re-compressing the image. HEIC and other formats are re-saved as high-quality JPG.

Will my photo turn sideways?

No. The orientation flag is kept so the photo displays the same way, while everything else is removed.

How do I stop my phone adding location to photos?

On iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Camera → Never. On Android: open the Camera app settings and turn off location tags.

Is it safe to use?

Yes. Your photos are processed on your own device and are never uploaded.
