Rotate PDF
Fix sideways pages

Turn scanned or sideways pages the right way up and save the PDF permanently.

    How to rotate a PDF

    1

    Add a PDF

    Drop a PDF onto the box above. Each page appears as a thumbnail.

    2

    Rotate pages

    Use Rotate all to turn every page, or the buttons under a page to rotate just that one.

    3

    Save

    Click Save PDF. The rotation is stored in the file, so it opens the right way everywhere.

    Permanent PDF rotation

    Rotating a page in a PDF viewer usually only changes the view — the next time you open the file, it's sideways again. Saving here writes the rotation into the PDF itself.

    Rotation is lossless: text stays selectable and nothing is re-compressed.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I rotate just one page?

    Use the rotate buttons under that page's thumbnail, then save.

    Does rotating a PDF reduce quality?

    No. Only the page's rotation setting changes; content is not re-compressed.

    Can I rotate and reorder pages together?

    Yes — use Organize PDF to rotate, reorder and delete pages in one go.

    Why does my scanned PDF have sideways pages?

    Scanners and phone scanning apps often save pages in the orientation the paper was fed or held. Rotating and saving here fixes the page orientation permanently in the file.

    Can I rotate a PDF on my phone?

    Yes. Rotate PDF works in mobile browsers such as Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android, with no app to install.
