Why convert WebP to JPG?

WebP is great for websites, but some apps, older software and upload forms still don't accept it. Converting to JPG gives you a file that opens everywhere — in photo editors, Office documents, printing services and older devices.

JPG doesn't support transparency, so any transparent areas in the WebP image are filled with white. If you need to keep transparency, convert WebP to PNG instead.

Frequently asked questions

Why can't I open a WebP file?

Some older programs and operating systems don't support WebP. Converting it to JPG makes it open in practically any app.

What happens to transparent areas?

JPG has no transparency, so transparent pixels are filled with a white background. Use the WebP to PNG converter to keep transparency.

Will the JPG be bigger than the WebP?

Often, yes — JPG is less efficient than WebP, so the same image usually needs a larger file. That's normal when converting for compatibility.
