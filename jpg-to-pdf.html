    How to convert JPG to PDF

    1

    Add images

    Drop your photos or scans onto the box above. You can add JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC and more.

    2

    Arrange and set up pages

    Drag the thumbnails into the right order. Choose the page size, orientation, margins and image quality.

    Images to PDF, made simple

    PDF is the standard way to send scanned documents, receipts, homework and photo sets as a single file. CompressZone places one image on each page, centred and scaled to fit, with automatic portrait or landscape orientation.

    Choose Balanced image quality for small, email-friendly PDFs, or High for printing. Everything happens on your device, so even sensitive documents like IDs and contracts stay private.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I combine multiple JPGs into one PDF?

    Add all the images at once, drag them into order, and click Create PDF. Each image becomes one page.

    Can I convert PNG or iPhone HEIC photos to PDF?

    Yes. PNG, WebP, HEIC, GIF and BMP images are all supported.

    How do I make the PDF smaller?

    Choose Small file under Image quality. You can also compress the photos first with the image compressor.

    Is it safe to convert ID documents?

    Yes. Your images are processed in your browser and are never uploaded to any server.
