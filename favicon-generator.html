What's in the favicon package

Browsers, phones and operating systems each look for different icon files. The package includes favicon.ico (16, 32 and 48 px in one file) for browsers, 16 and 32 px PNGs, a 180 px apple-touch-icon.png for iPhone home screens, 192 and 512 px Android icons, and a site.webmanifest.

The Apple touch icon always gets a solid background, because iOS shows transparent areas as black. If you upload an SVG, a favicon.svg is included too.

Frequently asked questions

What size should a favicon be?

Provide a 32 × 32 favicon for browser tabs, 180 × 180 for Apple devices and 192 and 512 for Android. This generator creates all of them from one image.

Do I still need favicon.ico?

Yes. Some browsers and tools still request /favicon.ico, so it's best to include it alongside PNG icons.

Can I use an SVG as a favicon?

Modern browsers support SVG favicons. Upload an SVG and the package includes favicon.svg plus PNG and ICO fallbacks.

Why does my favicon look blurry?

Start from a large, simple logo (512 px or more). Fine details and thin text become hard to see at 16 × 16 pixels.
