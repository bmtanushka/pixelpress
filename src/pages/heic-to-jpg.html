What is a HEIC file?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the format iPhones and iPads use for photos by default. It's about half the size of JPG at similar quality, but Windows, many Android apps and lots of websites can't open it.

CompressZone decodes HEIC on your device — natively in Safari, and with a built-in libheif decoder in other browsers — and saves a standard JPG. Your photos are never uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

Why can't I open HEIC files on Windows?

Windows needs an extra codec to display HEIC. Converting to JPG makes the photos open everywhere without installing anything.

Does converting HEIC to JPG lose quality?

At 80–90% quality the difference is invisible for normal viewing. The JPG will usually be larger than the HEIC, which is expected.

How do I stop my iPhone from saving HEIC?

On iPhone, go to Settings → Camera → Formats and choose Most Compatible. New photos will be saved as JPG.

Are live photos supported?

The still image of a Live Photo is converted. The motion part is not included.
