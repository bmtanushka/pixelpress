    Why PNG for PDF pages?

    PNG is lossless, so text, lines and charts stay perfectly crisp with no blur or color fringes. That makes it the best choice for slides, diagrams, documentation and anything you'll place in another document or presentation.

    PNG files are larger than JPG. For scanned photos or image-heavy pages, JPG is usually the better choice.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is PDF to PNG better than PDF to JPG?

    For text and diagrams, yes — PNG is sharper. For scanned photos, JPG gives much smaller files.

    Can I get a transparent background?

    Pages are rendered on white, as they appear in a PDF viewer.

    How do I insert a PDF page into PowerPoint or Word?

    Convert the page to PNG here, then insert the image into your document.

    How big will the PNG files be?

    At 150 DPI, a text page is usually 100–400 KB as PNG. Image-heavy pages and 300 DPI give larger files; choose JPG if size matters.

    Can I convert only some pages?

    All pages are converted, but each one has its own Download button, so you can save only the pages you need.
