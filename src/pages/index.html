Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. CompressZone runs entirely in your browser. Your images are read, compressed and saved on your own device — they are never sent anywhere.

What quality should I choose?

The default of 80% is the standard balance and works well for photos on websites and social media. Use 90% or higher for print or archiving, and 60% when the smallest file size matters most.

Does compressing an image reduce its quality?

JPG, WebP and AVIF compression is lossy, but at 80% quality the difference is usually invisible to the eye while the file is often 50–80% smaller. Use the before/after slider to check any image.

Why convert images to WebP?

WebP images are typically 25–34% smaller than JPG at the same visual quality, support transparency like PNG, and work in all modern browsers. They make websites load faster.

How does PNG compression work?

PNG is a lossless format, so CompressZone reduces the number of colors (color quantization) to shrink the file — often by 50–80% — while keeping transparency. At 95% quality or higher, PNGs are re-encoded losslessly.

Is there a file size or count limit?

There are no artificial limits on file size or the number of images. Very large images (over about 100 megapixels) may be limited by your device's memory.

Does it remove EXIF and location data?

Yes. Metadata such as camera details and GPS location is removed during compression, which is good for privacy. The image orientation is applied automatically first.

Does CompressZone work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. It works in any modern mobile browser, including Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android, and can be added to your home screen like an app.
