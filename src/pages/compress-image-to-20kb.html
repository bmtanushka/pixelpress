Tips for 20 KB uploads

A 20 KB limit is common for signatures and small photos on exam, admission and government application portals. At this size, every byte counts, so CompressZone combines a lower JPG quality with smaller dimensions when needed.

For best results, crop away empty background first, use a plain light background, and if the form specifies pixel dimensions (for example 200 × 230 or 140 × 60), enter them as an exact size so the photo isn't shrunk further than necessary.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my photo blurry at 20 KB?

20 KB is very small — about 1% of a typical phone photo. Cropping to just your face or signature and setting the exact pixel size the form needs gives the clearest result.

How do I make a signature under 20 KB?

Photograph or scan your signature on white paper, crop it tightly with the Crop Image tool, then compress it here. Signatures usually fit easily because they are mostly white.

Can I get an image between 10 KB and 20 KB?

Yes. The result is the highest quality that fits under 20 KB, which is usually 15–20 KB. For a smaller file, type a lower target such as 15 KB.
