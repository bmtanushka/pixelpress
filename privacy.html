Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy applies to compresszone.com ("CompressZone"), operated by Webulex Technologies.

Your files stay on your device

CompressZone processes images and PDF documents entirely inside your web browser. Your files are never uploaded, transmitted, stored or viewed by us or anyone else. When you close the page, all processed files are discarded from memory. Passwords you enter to open protected PDFs are used only in your browser.

Information we store locally

We save your compression preferences (such as format, quality and size settings) in your browser's local storage so they are remembered next time. A service worker caches the app files so the site loads quickly and works offline. You can clear both at any time in your browser settings.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show advertisements, which keeps CompressZone free. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Advertising scripts never have access to the files you process.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit CompressZone and which tools and pages are most useful. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as the pages you visit, your approximate location, your device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site. It never receives your files or their contents — those are processed only on your device.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can see how Google uses information from sites that use its services, and you can opt out of Google Analytics on all websites with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Where required by law, we ask for your consent before analytics and advertising cookies are used.

Children

CompressZone is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at contact@compresszone.com.
