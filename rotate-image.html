Why photos appear sideways

Cameras and phones often save photos in one orientation and add a hidden "orientation" tag to say how to display them. Some apps and websites ignore that tag, so the photo shows up sideways or upside down.

Rotating here saves the pixels in the correct orientation, so the image looks right everywhere — no hidden tag required.

Frequently asked questions

How do I rotate a photo 180 degrees?

Click Right (or Left) twice.

Does rotating reduce quality?

Rotating by 90° doesn't lose any pixels. Saving as JPG re-encodes the image; choose PNG or Best quality to avoid visible loss.

Why does my photo look correct on my phone but sideways on my computer?

Your phone reads the orientation tag but some software doesn't. Rotating and saving here fixes the pixels permanently.

Can I rotate an image by a custom angle, like 45°?

The tool rotates in 90° steps, which keeps every pixel sharp. Arbitrary angles would require cropping or adding background.

Can I rotate an iPhone HEIC photo?

Yes. HEIC photos open in every browser here and can be saved as JPG, PNG or WebP after rotating.
