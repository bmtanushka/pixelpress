    Why resize images?

    Resizing reduces the number of pixels in an image, which is the most effective way to cut file size. A 12-megapixel phone photo (4000 × 3000 px) contains far more detail than any screen can show. Scaling it to 1920 px wide reduces the pixel count by about 77% and typically shrinks the file by 80–95% after compression.

    Frequently asked questions

    What size should I resize images to for a website?

    For full-width images, 1920 px wide covers most screens. For images inside articles, 1200–1280 px is usually enough. Thumbnails can be 400–800 px.

    Does resizing keep the aspect ratio?

    Yes. The longest side is scaled to your chosen maximum and the other side is scaled proportionally, so images are never stretched or cropped.

    Will small images be enlarged?

    No. Images already smaller than the maximum dimension keep their original size; only the compression is applied.

    Can I resize and convert to WebP at the same time?

    Yes. Choose WebP as the output format and a maximum dimension, and both are applied in one step.
