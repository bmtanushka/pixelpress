When to convert JPG to PNG

Some design tools, apps and printing services require PNG files. Converting a JPG to PNG doesn't restore detail lost in the original JPG, but it prevents further loss when you edit and save the image again.

PNG files of photos are larger than JPGs. For a smaller file with optimized colors, keep the default 80% quality.

Frequently asked questions

Does converting JPG to PNG improve quality?

No. It can't restore detail lost by JPG compression, but it avoids further loss in future edits.

Why is the PNG bigger than the JPG?

PNG is lossless, so photos need much more data. Lower the quality to 80% to reduce colors and size.

Can I make the background transparent?

Converting doesn't remove backgrounds — JPGs have no transparency to begin with.

Can I convert many JPGs to PNG at once?

Yes. Add as many JPGs as you like and download them all as a ZIP.

Which is better for printing, JPG or PNG?

For photos, a high-quality JPG prints just as well and is much smaller. PNG is better for graphics, text and line art.
