Crop to fill vs. fit inside

When the shape of your image doesn't match the size you need, something has to give. Crop to fill scales the image to cover the whole frame and trims the edges evenly from the center — ideal for profile photos and thumbnails. Fit inside keeps the whole image and adds padding (white for JPG, transparent for PNG and WebP).

Images are never stretched or distorted. Combine an exact size with a target file size for strict upload forms.

Frequently asked questions

How do I resize an image to 600 × 600 pixels?

Enter 600 × 600 (or pick the passport preset), keep Crop to fill, and add your image. The center of the photo is kept.

Will my image be stretched?

No. CompressZone always keeps the original proportions and either crops the overflow or adds padding.

Can I make a small image bigger?

Yes — exact sizes can enlarge images, but upscaling can't add detail, so small images may look soft.

What padding color is used?

White for JPG (which has no transparency), and transparent for PNG and WebP.
