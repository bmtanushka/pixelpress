What is EXIF data?

EXIF (Exchangeable Image File Format) is hidden information that cameras and phones save inside every photo. It can include the camera model, lens, shutter speed, aperture, ISO, the date and time the photo was taken and — if location services were on — the exact GPS coordinates where you were standing.

That's useful for photographers, but it can reveal your home address when you share photos online. CompressZone reads the data locally and never uploads your photos.

Frequently asked questions

Can someone find my location from a photo?

Yes, if the photo contains GPS data. This tool shows whether it does — and you can remove it before sharing.

Do social networks remove EXIF data?

Most large social networks strip GPS data from public images, but messaging apps, email, cloud links and many websites keep the original file intact.

Why doesn't my photo show any metadata?

Screenshots, edited images and photos downloaded from social media often have their metadata removed already.
