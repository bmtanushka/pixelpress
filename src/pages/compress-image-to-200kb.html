Frequently asked questions

Is 200 KB good for website images?

Yes. Most large website images should be 100–200 KB or less for fast loading. Smaller thumbnails should be well under 50 KB.

How do I make a photo under 200 KB without losing quality?

Set the maximum dimension to 1920 px and keep the 200 KB target. At that size, the quality loss is typically invisible.

Can I compress PNG screenshots to 200 KB?

Yes. Choose PNG as the output format to keep the PNG type, or JPG/WebP for smaller files.
