What's new

  • 27 new tools. Compress to an exact size in KB (20, 50, 100, 200 KB), resize to exact pixels, passport photo and Instagram resizers, HEIC to JPG in every browser, PNG ⇄ JPG, image ⇄ Base64, crop, rotate and flip, a favicon generator, and an EXIF viewer and lossless remover.

  • Launch. CompressZone launches with a private, in-browser image compressor and WebP converter: adjustable quality (80% by default), batch processing, ZIP downloads, a before/after slider and offline support.
