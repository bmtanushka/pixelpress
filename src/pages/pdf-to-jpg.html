Choosing the right resolution

DPI (dots per inch) controls how many pixels each page gets. At 150 DPI, an A4 page becomes about 1240 × 1754 pixels — sharp on screens and small enough to share. At 300 DPI it's about 2480 × 3508 pixels, suitable for printing.

Pages are rendered with Mozilla's PDF.js engine, the same one used in Firefox, directly in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert only one page of a PDF to JPG?

Add the PDF and click Download under the page you want. Each page can be downloaded on its own.

What DPI should I choose?

150 DPI for screens, email and social media; 300 DPI for printing or documents with small text.

Can I convert a password-protected PDF?

Yes. You'll be asked for the password, which is only used in your browser.

Should I choose JPG or PNG?

JPG gives smaller files for scans and photos. PNG is lossless and best for pages with sharp text and diagrams.
