    Private PDF merging

    Merging PDFs usually means uploading contracts, bank statements or medical records to someone else's server. CompressZone combines the files directly in your browser using the open-source pdf-lib library, so they never leave your device.

    Pages are copied as they are, keeping text selectable, links working and page sizes unchanged. To merge only some pages, first extract them with Split PDF.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I merge PDFs in a specific order?

    Drag the files into the order you want before clicking Merge. The first file in the list comes first in the result.

    Does merging reduce quality?

    No. Pages are copied without re-compression, so text stays selectable and images keep their quality.

    Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

    Protected PDFs need to be unlocked first. They're marked in the list so you know which ones.

    Is there a limit on the number of files?

    No. The only limit is your device's memory.
