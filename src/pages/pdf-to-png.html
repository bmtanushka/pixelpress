Why PNG for PDF pages?

PNG is lossless, so text, lines and charts stay perfectly crisp with no blur or color fringes. That makes it the best choice for slides, diagrams, documentation and anything you'll place in another document or presentation.

PNG files are larger than JPG. For scanned photos or image-heavy pages, JPG is usually the better choice.
