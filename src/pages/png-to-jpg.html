Why convert PNG to JPG?

PNG is lossless, which makes photos saved as PNG very large. Converting them to JPG typically reduces the file size by 70–90%, making them easier to email and upload.

JPG doesn't support transparency, so transparent areas are filled with white. To keep transparency with a smaller file, convert PNG to WebP instead.

Frequently asked questions

What happens to the transparent background?

JPG can't store transparency, so transparent areas become white.

Is JPG lower quality than PNG?

JPG is lossy, but at 80–90% quality the difference is invisible for photos. For text and sharp graphics, use 90% or higher.

Should I convert screenshots to JPG?

For sharing, yes — the file is much smaller. For crisp text, keep 90%+ quality or use WebP.

How much smaller is JPG than PNG?

For photos, a JPG is usually 70–90% smaller than the same image saved as PNG. For simple graphics with few colors, the difference is smaller.

Can I convert PNG to JPG on iPhone or Android?

Yes. It works in any modern mobile browser — choose your PNGs from Photos or Files and download the JPGs.
