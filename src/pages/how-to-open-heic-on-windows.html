How to open HEIC photos on Windows — and convert them to JPG

Updated October 19, 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer: You have three options: install Microsoft's free HEIF Image Extensions (and the HEVC Video Extensions if photos still won't open), convert the photos to JPG in your browser with HEIC to JPG, or set your iPhone to transfer photos as JPG automatically.

Why Windows can't open HEIC files

Since iOS 11, iPhones save photos as HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container), which uses the HEVC codec to make files about half the size of JPG. Windows doesn't always include the codecs needed to display HEIC, so the Photos app may show an error or ask you to install an extension.

Option 1: Install the Microsoft extensions

Open the Microsoft Store and install HEIF Image Extensions. On many PCs you also need HEVC Video Extensions, which Microsoft sells for a small fee. After installing, HEIC photos open in the Photos app and show thumbnails in File Explorer.

Option 2: Convert HEIC to JPG in your browser

If you need to share, upload or edit the photos, JPG is the safest format. CompressZone's HEIC to JPG converter runs in Chrome, Edge or Firefox on Windows — no installation, no upload.

Option 3: Stop the problem at the source

On your iPhone, go to Settings → Photos → Transfer to Mac or PC and choose Automatic: photos are converted to JPG when copied to a computer by cable. To save new photos as JPG, go to Settings → Camera → Formats and choose Most Compatible.

Frequently asked questions

Is HEIC better than JPG?

HEIC files are roughly half the size of JPG at similar quality, but JPG works everywhere. Keep HEIC on your phone and convert to JPG when sharing.

Will converting HEIC to JPG reduce quality?

At 80–90% quality the difference isn't visible for normal viewing.

Can I convert HEIC to JPG without uploading my photos?

Yes. CompressZone decodes HEIC on your own computer, so your photos are never uploaded.
