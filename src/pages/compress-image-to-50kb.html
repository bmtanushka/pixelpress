When do you need a 50 KB photo?

50 KB is one of the most common upload limits for profile photos on job portals, scholarship and visa applications, and online forms. It's large enough for a clear head-and-shoulders photo at around 400–600 pixels wide.

CompressZone keeps the image as sharp as possible by choosing the highest quality that stays under 50 KB, and only reduces dimensions if there's no other way to fit.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reduce a photo from 2 MB to 50 KB?

Add it here with the 50 KB target on. A 2 MB photo needs to shrink by about 97%, so CompressZone lowers the quality and, if necessary, the dimensions automatically.

Will the photo still look good?

For a face photo shown at small sizes on a form, 50 KB looks clear. Use the before/after slider to check before you download.

My form needs JPG under 50 KB and 300 × 400 px. Can I do both?

Yes. Choose Max dimension → Exact size, enter 300 × 400 with Crop to fill, and keep the 50 KB target on.
