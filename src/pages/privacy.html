Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Your images stay on your device

CompressZone compresses and converts images entirely inside your web browser. Your images are never uploaded, transmitted, stored or viewed by us or anyone else. When you close the page, all processed images are discarded from memory.

Information we store locally

We save your compression preferences (format, quality and resize setting) in your browser's local storage so they are remembered next time. A service worker caches the app files so the site loads quickly and works offline. You can clear both at any time in your browser settings.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show advertisements, which keeps CompressZone free. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Advertising scripts never have access to the images you compress.

Analytics

We do not use analytics or tracking beyond what is required by our advertising partner described above.

Children

CompressZone is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at contact@compresszone.com.
