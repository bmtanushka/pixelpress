Frequently asked questions

Does converting WebP to PNG keep transparency?

Yes. PNG supports full transparency, and transparent areas in your WebP image are preserved.

Why is the PNG larger than the WebP?

WebP compresses more efficiently than PNG, so the PNG version is usually larger. Lower the quality to 80% to reduce the colors and file size.

Is the conversion lossless?

At 95% quality or higher, the PNG is a lossless copy of the decoded WebP image.
